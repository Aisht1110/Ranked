Privacy Policy for Kiddo Puzzle World

Last updated: June 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Kiddo Puzzle World ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection and usage practices for the Kiddo Puzzle World application.

2. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for children aged 2-7. We strictly adhere to the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

3. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers are collected.
  • No location data is tracked.
  • The app operates entirely offline and does not transmit data over the internet.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Kiddo Puzzle World does not contain third-party advertisements, analytics tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases. The app provides a safe, uninterrupted offline experience for children.

5. Device Permissions

Our app only requests the minimum necessary permissions required for the app to function on your device. We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically.
