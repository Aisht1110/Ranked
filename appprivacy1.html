01

Information We Collect

Cricket Live Scorer uses Firebase Authentication to manage secure user access. To provide core functionality — saving, scoring, and sharing cricket matches — we collect and store only the minimum data required:

  • Your email address (used for account identification and login)
  • A securely generated Firebase User ID (UID) unique to your account
  • Match data you create within the app (scores, teams, overs, results)

We do not collect background tracking data, precise location data, contact lists, device identifiers, or any data beyond what is required for the app to function.

02

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to operate and improve the app experience:

  • Authenticate your identity and manage secure login sessions
  • Link your created and saved cricket matches to your account
  • Enable match sharing and retrieval across sessions
  • Improve app reliability and fix bugs (anonymous, aggregated analytics only)

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetise your personal data with any third party. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

03

Data Storage & Security

All user authentication and match data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore & Firebase Auth), which is compliant with industry-standard security protocols. Data is stored on Google's servers and protected by encryption at rest and in transit.

While we take all commercially reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. If you suspect any unauthorised access to your account, please contact us immediately at asnstudios.app@gmail.com.

🔐 Firebase Auth uses secure token-based sessions. We never store raw passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Google Firebase.
04

Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. Choose the method that suits you:

⭐ Recommended

Method 1: Delete Inside the App

The fastest and instant way to delete your account and all data.

  • Open Cricket Live Scorer
  • Tap the menu icon (top-right)
  • Select "Delete Account"
  • Confirm deletion — all data is erased instantly
📧 Via Email

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you've uninstalled the app or can't access it, email us:

  • Email: asnstudios.app@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Send from your registered email address
  • Data erased within 7 business days
⚠️ Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, all match data, scores, and account information are permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

Children's Privacy

Cricket Live Scorer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at asnstudios.app@gmail.com and we will take steps to promptly delete such information.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at asnstudios.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 7–14 business days.
