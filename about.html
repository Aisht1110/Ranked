Our Story

Who we are

ASN Studios started as a passion project to solve a real problem: JEE aspirants lacked reliable, data-driven tools for college counselling. JEECounsellor.me was built to fill that gap — and has since served over 250,000 students across India with rank predictions, cutoff analysis, and college comparisons covering 264 institutions.

From there, we expanded into Android app development (CSAB Nexus), study material platforms (BTechBook.in), and automation infrastructure. Every product we ship is live, monetized, and used by real people — not prototypes or side experiments.

We're formally registered as an MSME Micro enterprise in India, operating under NIC Code 62011 (Computer Programming). Our work spans education technology, developer tooling, and business automation.
