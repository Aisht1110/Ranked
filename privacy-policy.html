Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025  ·  ASN Studios

Scope: This Privacy Policy applies to all products and services operated by ASN Studios, including CSAB Nexus (Android app), JEECounsellor.me, CSABCounselling.in, BTechBook.in, and ranked.icu (collectively, "the Services").

1. Who We Are

ASN Studios is a sole proprietorship IT company registered in India under the MSME Act (Udyam Registration, NIC Code 62011). Our registered address is 007 Bandwa, Sarila, Hamirpur, Uttar Pradesh 210431, India.

For privacy-related queries, contact us at: asnstudios.app@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account information: Name and email address when you sign up or contact us.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and device/browser information for analytics.
  • Payment data: For paid services (e.g., JEECounsellor.me, CSABCounselling.in, CSAB Nexus), payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store credit/debit card details. We may retain transaction IDs and amounts for accounting purposes.
  • App data (CSAB Nexus): Subscription status is verified via Google Play and Firestore. No sensitive personal data is stored beyond your subscription record.
  • Communication data: If you contact us via email or contact form, we retain your message to respond to your query.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Verify subscription or payment status
  • Respond to support requests and queries
  • Send transactional communications (receipts, access confirmations)
  • Monitor platform performance and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

Our services integrate with the following third parties, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Razorpay — Payment processing for subscriptions and one-time purchases. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • Google Play / Google Firebase (Firestore) — App distribution and database for CSAB Nexus. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics — Website traffic analytics (anonymized). Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Advertising on applicable free tiers of our apps. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad serving. Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase, Neon.tech PostgreSQL). We implement reasonable technical measures to protect against unauthorized access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access-controlled databases.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. If you request deletion of your data, we will remove it within 30 days except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records for tax purposes).

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at asnstudios.app@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Cookies & Tracking

Our websites may use cookies for session management and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly without them.
